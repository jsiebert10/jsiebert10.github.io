Featured Projects

Public Health · Agent-Based Simulation · M.S. Thesis

Designing TTA Strategies for Safe School Reopening in Chile

M.S. thesis (Univ. of Chile, highest honors) modeling schools as contact-network graphs and simulating COVID-19 spread via individual viral-load and infectiousness curves. Tested testing/tracing/isolation (TTA) policies across hundreds of scenarios: weekly antigen testing cut infections >50% while keeping in-person attendance above 90%, and combining it with reduced classroom capacity cut infections ~75–80% at under 5% online classes. Findings were used to design a national testing-pilot proposal for Chile's Ministry of Education and underpinned the Franz Edelman Award-winning initiative (INFORMS, 2022).
